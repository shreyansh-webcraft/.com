FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import my existing Excel data?

Yes, absolutely. We have a built-in Excel import tool that allows you to upload your existing customer and lead data in seconds.

Can multiple staff members use it at once?

Yes, the CRM is multi-user capable. You can create separate accounts for your sales team, managers, and admins, all working simultaneously.

Is my customer data secure?

Security is our top priority. We use enterprise-grade encryption and secure cloud servers to ensure your sensitive business data is fully protected and backed up.

Can I access the CRM from my mobile phone?

Yes, the entire platform is mobile-friendly. You can check your dashboard, add leads, or follow up with customers directly from your smartphone browser.

Do I need to install any software?

No installation is required. It is a 100% cloud-based web application. You just need a web browser and an internet connection to log in and start using it.
